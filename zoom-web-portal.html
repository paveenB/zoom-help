Manage Zoom at su.zoom.us

Use the web portal for cloud recordings, your profile and personal link, scheduled meetings, and account settings.

Open the Zoom web portal
Find a cloud recording
  1. Sign in at su.zoom.us.
  2. Open Recordings & Transcripts.
  3. Select a meeting to play, download, or share when those options are available.

Recordings can take time to process.

Update your profile
  1. Open Profile.
  2. Review your display name, picture, and meeting information.
  3. Some fields are managed by Shenandoah and cannot be changed.
Set a personal Zoom link
  1. Open Profile.
  2. Find Personal Link in the Meeting section.
  3. Select Customize, enter an available name, and save.

Availability depends on your Zoom license and university settings.

Review meeting settings
  1. Open Settings.
  2. Choose Meeting or Recording & Transcript.
  3. Use search to find a setting.

A locked setting is controlled by Shenandoah.

Request a Zoom Pro license

If your work requires Zoom Pro features, submit Shenandoah University's license request form.

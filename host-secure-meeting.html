For meeting hosts

Host and secure a meeting

Use the appropriate access controls, prepare participant permissions, and know where to find host tools during a meeting.

Before the meeting

Choose access securityUse a Waiting Room, passcode, or an approved authentication option.
Review sharingDecide whether only the host or all participants may share.
Add another hostAssign an alternative host or co-host when someone else will help manage the meeting.
Test firstCheck audio, video, captions, and presentation content before participants arrive.
Use Waiting Room appropriately
  • Use Waiting Room when you need to confirm who enters.
  • Admit expected participants from the Participants panel.
  • For recurring internal meetings, use the access option approved for the group.

Some Shenandoah settings may be locked and cannot be changed.

Manage participants
  • Open Participants to mute, rename, admit, or remove someone.
  • Use Host tools or Security to lock the meeting after everyone has arrived.
  • Do not post private meeting links publicly.
Allow someone to share
  1. Open the arrow beside Share or use Host tools.
  2. Choose who may share.
  3. Return sharing to Host only when participant sharing is no longer needed.
Find hidden host controls

When the meeting window is narrow, options such as Record, Captions, and additional host controls may appear under More.

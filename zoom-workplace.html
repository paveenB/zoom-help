Meeting controls

Start, join, or schedule
  • New Meeting: starts an instant meeting.
  • Join: joins with a meeting ID and passcode.
  • Schedule: creates a future meeting.
Check audio and video
  • Use the arrow beside Audio to select and test a microphone or speaker.
  • Use the arrow beside Video to select a camera.
  • Check computer permissions if a device is unavailable.
Share your screen
  1. Select Share.
  2. Choose a screen, window, or application.
  3. Select Share, then use Stop Share when finished.
Find additional controls

Recording, captions, and other features may appear under More when the meeting window is narrow.

Troubleshoot audio and video

People cannot hear me

  • Make sure you are not muted.
  • Select the arrow beside Audio and choose the correct microphone.
  • Open Audio Settings and use Test Mic.
  • Check the computer's microphone permission.

I cannot hear other people

  • Select the arrow beside Audio and choose the correct speaker.
  • Raise both the computer and Zoom volume.
  • Open Audio Settings and use Test Speaker.
  • Disconnect unused headphones, docks, or Bluetooth devices.

My camera is not working

  • Select the arrow beside Video and choose the correct camera.
  • Close other applications that may be using the camera.
  • Check the computer's camera permission.
  • Restart Zoom if the camera remains unavailable.

Zoom may need an update

  • Select your profile picture in Zoom Workplace.
  • Select Check for Updates.
  • Install the update and restart Zoom when prompted.

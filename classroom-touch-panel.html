Classroom Zoom Rooms

Zoom with separate room controls

The controller normally opens to the Zoom home screen. Join a scheduled meeting or use Contacts to connect to another room. Use the separate Shenandoah room controls only when you need to select a device or adjust the classroom camera or audio.

Choose how to connect

Join a scheduled meeting

  1. On the Zoom home screen, select the meeting from the room calendar.
  2. Tap Join.
  3. If the meeting is not listed, use its meeting ID or ask the meeting organizer for the correct invitation.

Instructors and meeting organizers may be responsible for scheduling their own meetings.

Connect directly to another room

  1. On the Zoom home screen, tap Contacts.
  2. Search for the other Zoom Room.
  3. Select the room and start the call.

Use this option when the rooms need to connect without a previously scheduled class meeting.

Only if needed

If the classroom system is off

Classroom systems are often turned on before use, so you may not see this screen. If the Shenandoah splash screen is displayed, touch anywhere to wake the system. Wait for the room controls to appear, select the appropriate room setup, and then open Zoom.

Important

Use Back to reach the room controls

When Zoom is open, press the Android Back button at the bottom of the controller to return to the Shenandoah room-control screen. Tap Zoom on that screen to return to the meeting.

Open the room controls when needed

You do not need to leave Zoom simply to join a meeting or contact another room. Follow these steps only when you need to select a presentation source or change room settings.

Return to room controlsPress Back if Zoom is visible. If the Shenandoah screen is already open, continue.
Open HomeTap Home on the Shenandoah control screen to begin the room setup.
Select ZoomChoose Zoom as the room activity.
Choose the locationSelect Here when the presenter is in this room, or Other Location when the presenter is elsewhere.

Select the presentation device

Presenter is here

  1. Select Here.
  2. Choose the room computer, Apple TV, or guest HDMI connection.
  3. Allow the room to open Zoom.

Presenter is elsewhere

  1. Select Other Location.
  2. Follow the room prompts for the remote presentation.
  3. Return to Zoom when the room is ready.

Share content in the room and meeting

Confirm the deviceMake sure the correct room computer, Apple TV, or guest HDMI source was selected.
Open ZoomTap Zoom on the room-control screen if the meeting controls are not visible.
Select Share ContentOpen Zoom’s sharing controls.
Share to MeetingSelect Share to Meeting so the content appears in the classroom and for remote participants.

Change the default camera view

Classroom cameras begin in a classroom-facing view. If the instructor is teaching from the room, press Back and use the separate room controls to select an instructor or presenter camera angle. These controls also adjust the room speakers and instructor microphone.

Return to the meeting

Tap Zoom on the room-control screen to return to Zoom’s meeting controls, sharing, captions, recording, View, and participant tools.

Quick check before class

The camera starts in classroom view. If the instructor is in the room, switch to an instructor or presenter angle. Then confirm the room microphone and speakers are not muted and the correct presentation device is visible.

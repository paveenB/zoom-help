Classrooms and conference rooms

Connect and present from a Mac

Choose the room computer, a wired USB-C or HDMI connection, or Apple TV. Then decide whether the content is only for the room or must also be shared in Zoom.

How are you presenting?

Room computer

Select the room computer or Mac mini on the touch panel, then open the presentation or meeting.

USB-C or HDMI

Connect the lectern or table cable to your Mac, then select the laptop or guest input.

Apple TV

Select Apple TV in Room Controls, then choose the room from Screen Mirroring on your Mac.

Use the room computer
  1. Wake the room controls.
  2. Select the room computer or Mac mini source.
  3. Open your presentation or Zoom meeting.
  4. Close personal files and sign out when finished.
Connect with USB-C or HDMI
  1. Connect the cable at the lectern or conference table.
  2. Use an appropriate adapter if the Mac does not have the required port.
  3. Open Room Controls and select the laptop, guest, USB-C, or HDMI source shown in that room.
  4. On the Mac, choose whether to mirror or extend the display.
Connect with Apple TV
  1. Open Room Controls and select Apple TV.
  2. On the Mac, open Control Center and select Screen Mirroring.
  3. Choose the Apple TV for the room.
  4. Enter the displayed code if prompted.
  5. Disconnect Screen Mirroring when finished.
Share through the Zoom app

You can share to the room directly from the Zoom Workplace Home screen. You do not need to start or join a meeting on the laptop.

If the room appears in Zoom

  1. Open Zoom Workplace and sign in.
  2. Select the detected Room shown near the top of the Zoom window.
  3. Choose Share screen.
  4. Select the screen or application when Zoom offers a choice.

Enter the room code or sharing key

  1. On the Zoom Workplace Home screen, select Share screen or Share.
  2. Enter the sharing key or room code displayed by the Zoom Room.
  3. Select the screen or application to share when prompted.
  4. Stop sharing from Zoom Workplace when finished.

The available button names may vary with the Zoom Workplace version and room settings.

This is screen sharing, not an extended display. The Zoom app sends a copy of the selected screen, desktop, or application to the room. It does not create a second macOS desktop for PowerPoint Presenter View.

If the image does not appear
  • Confirm the correct room source is selected.
  • Reconnect the cable or adapter.
  • Check Displays in macOS and choose Mirror or Extend.
  • For Apple TV, confirm that both the room display and Screen Mirroring are active.

Choose the correct Mac display mode

Mirror and Extend apply when macOS recognizes the room display as an additional display, such as through a wired connection or a supported AirPlay configuration. Sharing through the Zoom app does not create an extended display.

Simplest option

Mirror displays

The television or projector shows the same content as the Mac. Use this for straightforward presentations when everyone should see exactly what is on the laptop.

On the Mac, open Displays or Screen Mirroring and choose the option to mirror the built-in display.

Two-screen workspace

Extend the display

The television or projector becomes a second desktop. Drag the presentation window onto the room display. This can keep presenter notes or other windows on the Mac.

Only seeing a desktop background? The Mac is probably extending the display. Drag the presentation to the room screen or switch to Mirror.

Using Zoom from your laptop?

After connecting, confirm Zoom is using the intended microphone, speaker, and camera. Open the arrow beside Audio to choose the microphone and speaker, and the arrow beside Video to choose the camera.

Room devices vary

If the room provides a USB conferencing connection, choose the room camera, microphone, and speaker in Zoom. HDMI and Apple TV normally carry presentation content and sound, but do not automatically provide access to the room camera or microphone.

Choose a speaker
  • For sound through the room, select the room, display, HDMI, USB, or conferencing speaker shown in Zoom.
  • If sound remains on the Mac, check both Zoom's speaker selection and macOS Sound output.
  • Use Test Speaker before the meeting begins.
Choose a microphone and camera
  • Select the room microphone and camera only when the laptop is connected to the room's conferencing USB connection.
  • Otherwise, use the Mac's built-in microphone and camera or a connected external device.
  • Use Test Mic and preview the selected camera before participants arrive.

Show your content in the room

Traditional classroom display

On a classroom touch panel, selecting the room computer, laptop input, or Apple TV may display the source directly on the television or projector.

The exact source names vary by room.

Zoom Room

Use Share or Share Content

In a Zoom Room, select Share or Share Content to show the connected source—even when everyone is physically in the room and there are no remote participants.

Open Room Controls first when you need to choose the connected Mac mini, Apple TV, or guest USB-C source.

During a Zoom meeting: use Share or Share Content so both the room display and remote participants receive the presentation. For a local class, use View → Content only when that view is appropriate.

Account & sign-in help

Sign in to Canvas

Open your Canvas courses using your SUNet account and university single sign-on.

Open Canvas

Sign in with your university account

  1. Go to su.instructure.com.
  2. If prompted for Shenandoah single sign-on, enter your SUNet username with @su.edu, then your SUNet password.
  3. Complete your Authenticator verification if prompted. Follow the number shown for your own sign-in request.
  4. After signing in, open the course you need. If you already have an active SSO session, Canvas may open without asking you to sign in again.

Cannot sign in?

Check that you are using your university account. Use the password recovery option on the sign-in page or contact the IT Help Desk if you cannot continue.

iPad Help

Share your screen in a study or conference room and find the files you downloaded.

Getting started & common tasks

Open a guide to find files or share your screen in a room.

Share your iPad screen with Apple TV
  1. Turn on the room display, select its Apple TV input, and check the destination name.
  2. Swipe down from the top-right corner of your iPad to open Control Center. Tap Screen Mirroring.
  3. Select the room’s Apple TV and enter the onscreen code if requested.
  4. To disconnect, return to Control Center → Screen Mirroring → Stop Mirroring.

Use your institution’s approved network. Room-specific Wi-Fi and Apple TV names will be added to the finished guide.

Official support instructions

Find a downloaded file on iPad
  1. Open the Files app.
  2. Use Browse to check iCloud Drive and On My iPad. Look in Downloads for files saved from the web.
  3. Use Search if you know part of the file name. Open the file, then use its Share button to see available sharing options.

Official support instructions

Ready to disconnect?

Open Control Center, tap Screen Mirroring, then Stop Mirroring.

Need a hand?

Include the room name and what appears on your iPad when contacting support.

Sign in directly to Shenandoah Panopto

To work on the Panopto website, follow the steps below. To access course videos within Canvas, use the Panopto in Canvas guide.

Open PanoptoGo to su.hosted.panopto.com.
Choose CanvasSelect Canvas from the dropdown, then select Sign in.
Complete SSO if promptedFollow the Shenandoah single sign-on prompts using your university account.
Authorize if promptedIf Canvas asks to allow Panopto access, confirm it shows your university account, then select Authorize.

Which login option? Choose Canvas for regular university access. The Panopto option is for administrators.

First time signing in?

You may see an SSO sign-in or a Canvas authorization prompt, especially on your first visit. These prompts may also appear again later.

Canvas authorization

Check the account shown before selecting Authorize. The prompt may also offer “Remember my authorization for this service.” If the account is wrong or you cannot continue, contact the Help Desk.

Sign inOpen Shenandoah Panopto and choose Canvas to sign in.
Check your sourcesConfirm the microphone, camera, screen, and destination folder.
Record and reviewMake a short test and check the sound and picture.
Check accessConfirm permissions before sharing your video link.
Plan your first Panopto recording
  1. Open su.hosted.panopto.com, choose Canvas, and complete any sign-in prompts. Then choose the recording tool available to you.
  2. Confirm the destination folder and the camera, microphone, and screen you intend to capture. Make a short test first.
  3. After recording, let the upload finish. Review the video and its sharing permissions before sending a link.

Sample recording overview. Detailed recording screenshots and sharing instructions will be added for the Shenandoah setup.

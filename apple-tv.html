Connection & troubleshooting guides

Select a topic to see the instructions.

Connect your Mac to Apple TV
  1. Wake the room display and select the Apple TV input using the room controls. Check the room label so you choose the right destination.
  2. On your Mac, open Control Center in the menu bar, then Screen Mirroring. Select the room’s Apple TV.
  3. If a code appears on the room display, enter it on your Mac. Follow any onscreen sharing choices.
  4. Choose mirroring for the same content on both screens, or extend to use a separate workspace. When finished, end the connection in Screen Mirroring.

Campus networks may handle AirPlay differently. This sample needs your approved Wi-Fi instructions and room naming conventions before rollout.

Official support instructions

Mirror or extend your Mac display
  1. Connect to the room display, then open Apple menu → System Settings → Displays.
  2. Select the connected display. Use the “Use as” setting to choose mirroring or an extended display; labels vary by macOS version.
  3. For an extended desktop, use Arrange when available to match the screens’ physical positions. Move a window toward the edge that leads to the other display.

Mirror is useful when everyone needs to see exactly what you see. Extend gives you separate space for notes and shared content.

Official support instructions

The TV only shows my wallpaper
  1. Your Mac may be using the room screen as an extended desktop. The connection can be working even when no window is on that screen.
  2. To show the same content on both screens, open System Settings → Displays, select the room display, and choose mirroring.
  3. To keep an extended desktop, move the window to the room screen. Check the display arrangement if you cannot tell which edge to use.

Official support instructions

Share your iPad screen with Apple TV
  1. Turn on the room display, select its Apple TV input, and check the destination name.
  2. Swipe down from the top-right corner of your iPad to open Control Center. Tap Screen Mirroring.
  3. Select the room’s Apple TV and enter the onscreen code if requested.
  4. To disconnect, return to Control Center → Screen Mirroring → Stop Mirroring.

Use your institution’s approved network. Room-specific Wi-Fi and Apple TV names will be added to the finished guide.

Official support instructions

Arrange displays and find a missing window
  1. Open System Settings → Displays while connected to the room display.
  2. Open Arrange if available and drag the display thumbnails to match their physical positions.
  3. Move your pointer across the corresponding screen edge, then drag the window onto the screen you want. If presenting is urgent, switch to mirroring.

Official support instructions

Choose where your sound plays
  1. Check that the video or app is not muted.
  2. Open System Settings → Sound → Output and select the intended available output.
  3. Check the room display’s volume and mute setting. Play a short test before the meeting begins.

Available audio outputs depend on your connection and room equipment. Confirm the local setup before sharing this guide.

Official support instructions

Apple TV missing from the list?

Check the room display is awake and shows the correct input. Follow the approved campus network instructions or contact the Help Desk.

Before you leave

End Screen Mirroring on your device and confirm your content is no longer on the room screen.

Accessibility and meeting records

Recordings, captions, and transcripts

Use live captions for real-time accessibility and manage recordings or retained transcripts from the Zoom web portal.

Live captions

Captions display spoken words during the meeting. Select Captions or look under More when the control is not visible.

Spoken language may be set by Shenandoah and may not be changeable.

Recordings and transcripts

A recording preserves meeting media. A transcript is retained text and is different from live captions.

Availability depends on the host's license and university settings.

Start a recording
  1. Tell participants when a meeting will be recorded.
  2. Select Record. If it is hidden, select More.
  3. Choose the available recording option.
  4. Pause or stop the recording when appropriate.
Enable live captions
  1. Select Captions or More.
  2. Enable captions when applicable.
  3. Confirm captions appear for participants who need them.
Find a cloud recording
  1. Sign in at su.zoom.us.
  2. Open Recordings & Transcripts.
  3. Select the meeting and wait for processing to finish if necessary.
Share a recording
  1. Open the recording's sharing settings.
  2. Choose the intended audience and available security options.
  3. Add authorized viewers by email when required.
  4. Review what viewers may download or see before sharing.

Protect course and meeting information

Share recordings only with the intended audience and follow Shenandoah requirements for student, personnel, or confidential information.

Conference room Zoom Room

Connect, share, or start a meeting

Use this guide in conference rooms with a Logitech Tap controller and Rally Bar. For most local presentations, connect the provided cable and share your laptop.

Most common task

Share your laptop in the room

Use the provided USB-C cable. It carries your laptop’s video into the room through an HDMI connection.

Connect and share

Connect the cablePlug the provided USB-C cable into your laptop.
Check the room displayYour screen may appear automatically after a few seconds.
Select Share if neededOn the Tap, select Share or Share Content if the laptop does not appear.
Disconnect when finishedUnplug the cable so the room is ready for the next user.
Connection note: This USB-C cable is used for video through the room’s HDMI connection. Do not rely on it to charge your laptop.

Start or join a Zoom meeting

Start the meeting

  1. Select a scheduled meeting, New Meeting, or Join.
  2. Follow the prompts shown on the Tap.
  3. The Rally Bar normally provides the room camera, microphone, and speakers.

Present your laptop

  1. Connect the provided USB-C cable.
  2. Select Share or Share Content if needed.
  3. Choose the content you want participants to see.

During the meeting

Use the Zoom controls on the Tap to mute or unmute, start or stop video, share content, and adjust the camera when those controls are available.

When the meeting ends

Select End and confirm that you want to end or leave the meeting. Disconnect your laptop before leaving.

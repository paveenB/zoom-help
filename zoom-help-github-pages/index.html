Know which Zoom you are using

The desktop client is for meetings. The web portal is for recordings, profile information, scheduling, and account settings.

Sign in to the desktop client

1

Open Zoom Workplace

Launch the Zoom application on your computer.

2

Choose Sign in with SSO

Do not create a separate Zoom account.

3

Enter the domain

Enter su when Zoom asks for the company domain.

4

Use your SU account

Complete the Shenandoah sign-in in your browser.

Meetings

Join, start, and participate

These are the controls most people need during a typical meeting.

Join a meeting
  1. Select the meeting link in your email or calendar.
  2. If prompted, allow the Zoom Workplace app to open.
  3. Confirm your name, audio, and video before joining.

You can also select Join in the desktop client and enter a meeting ID and passcode.

Start or schedule a meeting
  • New Meeting: starts an instant meeting.
  • Meetings: shows your upcoming meetings; select one and choose Start.
  • Schedule: creates a future meeting and invitation.
Microphone and speakers
  • Select Mute or Unmute.
  • Select the arrow beside the microphone to choose a microphone or speaker.
  • Open Audio Settings to test both devices.
Camera and screen sharing
  • Select Start Video or Stop Video.
  • Select the arrow beside Video to choose a camera or background.
  • Select Share, choose a screen or window, and then select Share.
  • Select Stop Share when finished.
Captions, chat, and participants
  • Captions: show spoken words as text when available.
  • Chat: opens meeting messages.
  • Participants: shows the participant list and common host controls.
  • More: contains additional features that may not fit on the toolbar.
Leave or end a meeting
  • Leave Meeting: exits while the meeting continues.
  • End Meeting for All: stops the meeting for everyone; this option is for the host.
Check before ending: hosts should use Leave if someone else needs to continue the meeting.
Settings

Fix common audio and video issues

Open your profile picture in the desktop client and select Settings.

People cannot hear me

  • Make sure you are not muted.
  • Choose the correct microphone.
  • Use Test Mic in Audio Settings.
  • Check the computer's microphone permission.

I cannot hear others

  • Choose the correct speaker.
  • Raise the computer and Zoom volume.
  • Use Test Speaker in Audio Settings.
  • Disconnect unused headphones or docks.

My camera is not working

  • Choose the correct camera.
  • Close other applications using the camera.
  • Check the computer's camera permission.
  • Restart Zoom if the camera remains unavailable.

Update Zoom

  • Select your profile picture in the desktop client.
  • Select Check for Updates.
  • Install the update and restart Zoom when prompted.
Web portal

Recordings, profile, and account settings

Sign in at su.zoom.us. Available options depend on your Zoom license and university settings.

Find a cloud recording
  1. Open su.zoom.us and sign in.
  2. Open Recordings & Transcripts.
  3. Select a meeting to play, download, or share it when those options are available.

A new recording may take time to process. A recording started by a co-host normally appears under the meeting host's account.

Update your profile
  1. Open the web portal and select Profile.
  2. Review your display name, profile picture, and meeting information.
  3. Some fields are managed by Shenandoah and cannot be changed.
Set a personal Zoom link

Your personal Zoom link is sometimes called a Zoom alias.

  1. Open Profile.
  2. Find Personal Link in the Meeting section.
  3. Select Customize, enter an available link name, and save.

This option may not be available for every account.

Review meeting settings
  1. Open Settings in the web portal.
  2. Choose the Meeting or Recording & Transcript section.
  3. Use search when available to find a setting.

A locked option is controlled by Shenandoah's Zoom administrators.

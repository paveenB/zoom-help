Google Calendar

Schedule a Zoom Room

First reserve the physical room. Then choose whether the event should create a new Zoom meeting ID or use your existing personal Zoom link.

Recommended

Create a new Zoom meeting ID

  1. Create the Google Calendar event.
  2. Add the physical room using Rooms.
  3. Under Add video conferencing, select Zoom Meeting.
  4. Save and send the event.
  5. Confirm the meeting appears on the room controller.

Use this for classes, separate meetings, and events involving different groups.

Personal room

Use your personal Zoom link

  1. Create the Google Calendar event.
  2. Add the physical room.
  3. Do not create another Zoom Meeting through the add-on.
  4. Paste the complete personal Zoom link into Location.
  5. Save the event and confirm Start or Join appears on the controller.

Use this for trusted groups that meet with you regularly.

Avoid conflicting links: do not create a new Zoom meeting with the add-on and then replace it with your personal link.

The room and the meeting are separate

Adding the physical room reserves the space. Adding Zoom conferencing provides the meeting connection.

Check before the meeting

Open the room controller’s meeting list and confirm the event displays a usable Start or Join control.

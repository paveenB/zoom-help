Panopto & Canvas

Use Panopto in Canvas

Access Panopto videos from your Canvas course, including videos embedded in course pages or modules.

Open Canvas

Open your course videos

Sign in to CanvasOpen su.instructure.com and sign in with your university account if prompted.
Open your courseChoose the course whose videos you want to view.
Find PanoptoLook for the course’s Panopto link in its navigation. It may be labeled Panopto Video; the exact name can vary.
Open a videoSelect the course video you need. If your instructor embedded a video on a Canvas page or in a module, open it there instead.

Need help signing in to Canvas?

Asked to sign in or authorize?

Follow any university sign-in prompts. If Canvas asks you to authorize Panopto, check that the displayed account is yours before continuing.

Panopto link or video missing?

Check the course pages and modules for video links. Ask your instructor where the course videos are located if you cannot find them.

Course videos and personal Zoom recordings are different

Videos in your Canvas course

Start in the Canvas course to find the videos made available there. What you can see depends on the course content and your access.

Faculty & staff Zoom recordings

Start at the Panopto website, then open My Folder → Zoom Recordings. Finding a recording in this personal folder does not mean it is available to students in a Canvas course.

Find a Zoom recording
I see an access error

Confirm you are signed in with the university account enrolled in the course. If the video still will not open, give your instructor or the Help Desk the course name, video title, and the error message.

Faculty: I want students to see a recording

Confirm that the intended students have access and that the video is linked or embedded in the course. Do not assume that the transfer from Zoom to your personal Panopto folder publishes it to Canvas.

Contact the Help Desk if you need assistance placing the video in your course.
